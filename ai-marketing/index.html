FAQ

Questions agencies ask us.

Who pays for the ad spend?
You do, directly to TikTok. Our 10% fee is on top of the ad budget and only covers our work: strategy, creative, optimization and reporting.

We recommend starting around AED 5,000 a month so the ads gather enough data to optimize. We can discuss your specific situation on a call.

You do not have to, but the two are built to work together. Marketing drives the traffic, the agent answers and books it. Without the agent, the leads go to your team.
Can we pause or stop?
Yes. It is month to month with no lock-in. You can pause the service or lower the ad budget whenever you want.
How fast do we see results?
The ads start delivering traffic within the first week. The first meaningful cost-per-lead numbers usually show after one to two weeks of optimization.
